About Us

NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION

Rainbow Roof Gallery is a non-profit arts organization located in Oakville, Canada, supporting cultural connection and artistic growth within the local community.

Through art classes, workshops, and cross-border artist exchanges, we strive to provide an open space where anyone can enjoy and experience art in their daily lives.
